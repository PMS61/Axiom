$y$ & Binary recall outcome of a review: $1$ if the rating is greater than 1, else $0$\\
$\Delta t$ & Elapsed time in days between two reviews (clipped to $[0.01, 365]$)\\
$\psi_{n,t}$ & Latent state (logit scale) of card sequence $n$ at review $t$\\
$\varphi$ & OU decay factor, $\varphi = \exp(-\lambda \Delta t)$\\
$q$ & OU transition noise variance, $q = \sigma^2(1-e^{-2\lambda\Delta t})/(2\lambda)$\\
$\lambda$ & OU mean-reversion rate ($\lambda_{n,t}$ when practice-dependent)\\
$\kappa$ & Practice coefficient in $\lambda_{n,t} = \lambda\exp(-\kappa C_{n,t})$\\
$C_{n,t}$ & $\log(1 + \text{number of successful recalls before review } t)$\\
$\sigma^2$ & OU diffusion (volatility) parameter\\
$\mu_{n,t}$ & Equilibrium mean, $\mu_{n,t} = b_0 + \alpha_u + \beta_i + \gamma_u X_{n,t}$\\
$X_{n,t}$ & Lagged outcome: $y$ of the previous review of the same card (0 at first review)\\
$b_0$ & Global intercept\\
$\alpha_u$ & User effect\\
$\beta_i$ & Item (card-level) effect\\
$\gamma_u$ & User-specific coefficient on the lagged outcome\\
$g_0$ & Hyper-mean of $\gamma_u$\\
$\tau^2$ & Hierarchical variance hyperparameter (e.g.\ $\tau^2_\alpha$, $\tau^2_\beta$, $\tau^2_\gamma$)\\
$\omega$ & P\'olya-Gamma auxiliary variable, $\omega \sim \mathrm{PG}(1,\psi)$\\
$z$ & Pseudo-observation, $z = (y - \tfrac{1}{2})/\omega$\\
$C$ & Shorthand for $C_{n,t}$ in the rate formula (defined above)\\
$\hat{R}$ & Potential scale reduction factor\\
$k_{n,t}$ & P\'olya--Gamma numerator $k_{n,t} = y_{n,t} - \tfrac{1}{2}$\\
$L$ & Number of terms in the truncated P\'olya--Gamma series ($L = 200$)\\
$K_{n,t}$ & Kalman gain of the FFBS filter (distinct from $L$)\\
$N$ & Total number of review events\\
$N_{\mathrm{tr}}$ & Number of transition terms ($t \ge 2$) in the $\lambda$ and $\sigma^2$ updates\\
$U$, $I$ & Numbers of users and items\\
$G$ & Number of groups in a hierarchical effect (in the $\tau^2$ update)\\
$S$ & Number of posterior draws used for prediction (at most 100)\\
$Q(\lambda)$ & Half sum of squared standardised residuals used in the $\lambda$ and $\sigma^2$ conditionals\\
$d_{n,t}$ & Transition design, $1-\varphi_{n,t}$\\
$v_{n,t}(\lambda)$ & Variance factor $(1-e^{-2\lambda\Delta t})/(2\lambda)$\\
$\mathrm{logistic}(\psi)$ & Logistic function $1/(1+e^{-\psi})$\\
$a_{n,t}$ & FFBS predicted mean of the state before review $t$ is observed, $a_{n,t} = \varphi_{n,t}\,m_{n,t-1} + (1-\varphi_{n,t})\,\mu_{n,t}$\\
$m_{n,t}$ & FFBS filtered mean of $\psi_{n,t}$\\
$P_{n,t}$ & FFBS filtered variance of $\psi_{n,t}$\\
$R_{n,t}$ & FFBS predicted variance of $\psi_{n,t}$ before the observation\\
$K_{n,t}$ & FFBS gain (listed above)\\
$J_{n,t}$ & Backward-pass gain of the FFBS smoother\\
$h_{n,t}$ & Mean of the backward-pass conditional draw of $\psi_{n,t}$\\
$H_{n,t}$ & Variance of the backward-pass conditional draw of $\psi_{n,t}$\\
$r_{n,t}$ & Transition residual $r_{n,t} = \psi_{n,t} - \varphi_{n,t}\,\psi_{n,t-1}$ in the effects regression\\
$\bar\omega_{n,t}$ & Mean of the P\'olya--Gamma weight, $\bar\omega = \tanh(a/2)/(2a)$, used in prediction\\
$N_{\mathrm{seq}}$ & Number of card sequences (rows of the padded FFBS array)\\
$T_{\max}$ & Length of the longest sequence (columns of the padded FFBS array)\\
\end{longtable}
