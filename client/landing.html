The Structural Failure

The problem isn't access to content. It's the mismatch between demand and availability.

01. Fixed Formats

Textbooks and videos treat every learner identically. If the format doesn't match your brain's absorption pattern, you don't fail—the system does.

02. Blind Scheduling

Calendars ask when you are free, not when you are ready. Learning Dynamic Programming at 11PM after a full day isn't a discipline problem—it's a system design error.

03. The Disconnect

Your learning roadmap and your daily schedule don't talk. Your scheduler doesn't know the cognitive cost of a new concept, and your course doesn't know you just hit a 4PM trough.

Complete Transparency

No hidden algorithms.
No black boxes.

Axiom doesn't "suggest" or "optimize" behind the scenes. When two things need to happen at once, it uses strict rules to figure out what goes where, and shows you exactly how it made that choice.

How Axiom handled a conflict at 2:00 PM
1. Energy cost of "Write Report": Very High (9/10)
2. Available energy at 1:00 PM: Low
3. RULE: Do not place high-focus tasks in low-energy slots.
4. Next available high-energy slot: 2:00 PM
5. CONFLICT: "Team Sync" is already at 2:00 PM
6. RULE: If two tasks clash, the one requiring more focus gets the slot.
7. → ACTION: Place "Write Report" at 2:00 PM.
8. → ACTION: Move "Team Sync" to 3:30 PM.
