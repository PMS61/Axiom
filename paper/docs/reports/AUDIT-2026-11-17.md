# Independent cross-verification (2026-11-17)

Two auditors worked independently and did not reuse the project's metric, report or export code.
Their scripts are in the session scratchpad (auditA/, auditB/) and are not part of the repo.

## Audit B: numbers
- Every headline number reproduces from the raw prediction and event files within 0.0005. This covers:
  - all models' log loss, Brier, AUC and ECE on Anki, Duolingo and seeds 1 and 2;
  - the three constants;
  - the paired point differences;
  - the user-win shares (82/100 and 32,151/41,223);
  - the srs-benchmark means. All 1,000 of 1,000 users match on size.
- 27 numbers taken from the PDF text all match.
- Bootstrap CI endpoints in a fresh 500-resample bootstrap differ by up to 0.002 on the Anki comparisons against the constant. This is resampling noise: no CI changes its significance.

## Audit A: leakage (empirical)
- **Future-label flips:**
  - Anki, 50 probes: 35,442 earlier predictions show 0 changes after a flip, while later predictions do change.
  - Duolingo, same-session flips: the predicted row shows 0 changes.
  - srs-benchmark driver, 20 probes: earlier predictions show 0 changes.
- **Training rows:** GBDT fits on exactly the `is_train & ~is_first` rows, with no cutoff violations.
- **Shuffled test labels:** permuting them makes the log loss against the true labels worse (0.3584 to 0.4405). The model cannot exploit the shuffled labels.
- **F1 (MEDIUM, disclosed):** the Duolingo lexeme rate pools training rows from all users. In a 300-user sample, 19.5% of those source rows are calendar-later than the scored row. The effect is negligible: log loss 0.33316 with the feature vs 0.33292 without, on the sample. Full-data ablation gives about 0.001 (EVALUATION 11.4(e), limitations (e)).
- **F2 (LOW):** `results/paper_duolingo/events.parquet` has no `ts` or lexeme columns. Rebuilding GBDT from it silently falls back to day grouping. Rebuild from the raw csv.gz.

**Verdict:** there is no per-row leak. The one cross-user, calendar-time effect is already disclosed and is about 0.001 log loss.
